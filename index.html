About Us

One studio, three things done properly

Digital Studio started with a simple idea: students shouldn't have to piece their notes together from ten different places, and job aspirants shouldn't have to guess what actually gets asked in the exam. We build clear, exam-ready study material for University of Gour Banga students — covering Political Science and Bengali across Major, Minor and MDC papers, semester by semester.

Alongside that, we run focused classes, notes and quizzes for major central government exams — SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD, Stenographer, Selection Post, and Railway exams including RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP, JE, Technical, and RPF SI & Constable.

And when you need something built, designed or printed — a business website, a birthday website, a school or college site, ID cards, certificates, marksheets, or a presentation — that's the third leg of the studio, done at prices that make sense for students and small businesses alike.
