About the University

University of Gour Banga

Established in 2008, University of Gour Banga (UGB) is a state university located in Malda, West Bengal, set up to expand higher education access across North Bengal. A number of affiliated degree colleges across the region conduct undergraduate and postgraduate teaching under the university, following its syllabus and examination system — which is exactly what these notes are built around.
